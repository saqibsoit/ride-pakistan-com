# Cancellation and Refund Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy applies to all bookings made on ride-pakistan.com, run by Destination Pakistan ("we", "us"). All prices are in Pakistani rupees (PKR).

## 1. Single and private sessions

- **Cancel at least 48 hours before your session:** you get a full refund to your original payment method, or account credit if you prefer. Message us on WhatsApp or email hello@ride-pakistan.com with your booking reference.
- **Cancel less than 48 hours before, or don't turn up:** no refund or credit. Your trainer and horse have been reserved for you.
- **Late arrival:** your session still ends at its scheduled time.
- **No helmet:** riders must bring their own riding helmet. A rider without one can't ride, and this counts as a no-show.

## 2. Packages (8 and 16 classes)

- Each package session is booked with a credit from your package.
- **Cancel a package session at least 48 hours before:** the credit returns to your package.
- **Cancel less than 48 hours before, or don't turn up:** the credit is used.
- Packages must be used within their validity period (6 weeks for 8 classes, 10 weeks for 16 classes) from the date of purchase. Unused credits expire at the end of that period and are not refunded.
- **Changed your mind?** If you haven't used any session, you can cancel a package within 7 days of purchase for a full refund. After that, packages are non-refundable.

## 3. Private session requests

When you request a private session at a custom date and time, nothing is charged until we approve it. After approval you have 2 hours to pay. If you don't pay in time, the request lapses and you owe nothing. Once paid, section 1 applies.

## 4. If we cancel or change a session

We may cancel or move a session because of weather, safety, the condition of the horses, or trainer availability. If that happens, you choose one of these:

- move to another available session at no cost;
- account credit (package bookings get their credit back); or
- a full refund of what you paid for that session.

If we change the time of your session, we email you with links to accept the new time, move to another session, or cancel. If you don't respond, your booking stays at the new time.

## 5. Gift vouchers

- Vouchers are valid for 12 months from the date they are issued and can't be exchanged for cash.
- An unused voucher can be refunded within 14 days of purchase. After that, vouchers are non-refundable.
- If a booking paid partly or fully with a voucher is refunded, the voucher share goes back to the voucher (or to account credit if the voucher has expired).

## 6. Discounts, credit and promo codes

Refunds cover the amount you actually paid. Any share paid with account credit or a gift voucher is returned to that credit or voucher. Promo code and referral discounts are not paid out in cash.

## 7. How refunds are paid

- **Card payments:** refunded to the same card. Your bank usually shows it within 5 to 10 working days.
- **Bank transfers:** refunded by bank transfer to the account you paid from, within 7 working days of our approval.
