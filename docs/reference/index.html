Two hours,
start to finish.

This isn’t a pony ride around a ring. You’ll do everything a horseman does, from bringing the horse in to settling him down after the ride. That’s how real trust is built.

  1. Bring him in

    Walk out to the paddock and lead your horse in. You’ll learn how to approach a horse, where to stand and how to read his mood.

  2. Groom

    Brush him down and check his hooves. It’s the quiet part of the session, and it’s where the bond starts.

  3. Tack up

    Fit the saddle and bridle yourself, with your trainer checking every buckle.

  4. Ride

    Mostly trail riding, out in the open. If you’d rather work in the arena on your position and control, that’s possible too.

  5. Cool down

    Untack, then give your horse a massage and stretch. You finish the way you started: looking after him.

Meet Omar Saleem.

Omar was born into a polo family. His father captained the Pakistan Army polo team and hoped his sons would form a polo team of their own.

Omar chose a different path. He believes competitive equestrian sport turns horses into equipment and breaks the bond between horse and rider. His focus is the older relationship: trail, endurance and cross-country riding, covering new ground together.

A horse is a prey animal. He wants to explore, but he’s wary. Once he trusts the rider on his back, he relaxes and becomes brave enough to go anywhere. Every session is built around earning that trust.

Win the horse’s trust, and he’ll take you anywhere.

Questions.

I’ve never been near a horse. Is that okay?

Yes. The sessions are made for complete beginners. You’ll spend time on the ground with your horse before you ever get in the saddle, and your trainer stays with you throughout.

What should I wear?

Full-length trousers and closed shoes or boots with a small heel are best. Avoid loose scarves and anything that could catch on the saddle.

Can I book a private session?

Yes. Private sessions are available for individuals, couples, families and small groups. Send us a DM with your dates.

Where exactly are you?

We’re in Islamabad. We share the exact location pin when we confirm your booking.

Do the timings ever change?

Our current slots are 8–10 am and 3–5 pm on Saturdays and Sundays. They shift slightly with the seasons, and we’ll always confirm your slot in advance.
