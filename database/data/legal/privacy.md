# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information Ride Pakistan collects, why, and your choices. Ride Pakistan is run by Destination Pakistan, Plot No. 68, Siddique Trade Centre, Gulberg, Lahore ("we", "us"). Contact: hello@ride-pakistan.com.

## 1. What we collect

- **Account and contact details:** your name, email address and WhatsApp number. If you book without signing in, we use the account that already belongs to your email address, or create one, so your bookings, tickets and receipts stay together.
- **Rider details:** each rider's name, age group, riding experience, notes you give us, and an emergency contact. Emergency contacts and notes are stored encrypted.
- **Bookings and payments:** your bookings, orders, package credits, gift vouchers and payment records. Card payments are handled by Stripe. We never see or store your full card number.
- **Bank transfer proof:** if you pay by bank transfer, the screenshot you upload. It is kept privately and deleted 90 days after we review it. The payment record itself is kept for our accounts.
- **Enquiries:** messages you send through our forms, and details of how you found us (for example the advert or link you came from).
- **Session records:** check-ins, the horse assigned to you, and short progress notes from your trainer.
- **Technical information:** basic logs needed to run and secure the website, such as IP address and browser type.

## 2. Why we use it

- To take and manage your bookings, payments, refunds and tickets.
- To send booking confirmations, reminders, schedule changes and other service messages by email and WhatsApp.
- To email you a private link for each booking, so you can see your tickets, pay and make changes without signing in. Anyone with the link can see that booking. It stops working 7 days after your last session, and you can stop it at any time from the link or your account.
- To keep riders safe, for example by sharing rider details and emergency contacts with your trainer.
- To answer your questions and follow up on enquiries.
- To keep our accounts and meet legal and tax requirements.
- To protect the website against fraud and abuse.
- With your consent, to measure our advertising (see section 5).

We do not sell your personal information.

## 3. Who we share it with

Only with the services we need to run Ride Pakistan, and only for that purpose:

- **Stripe** (card payments)
- **Resend** (sending emails)
- **Hostinger** (website hosting) and **Cloudflare** (security, performance and spam protection on forms)
- **Our trainer** (the rider details needed for your session)
- **Meta and Google**, only if you accept analytics cookies (see section 5)

We may also share information when the law requires it.

## 4. How long we keep it

- Account and booking information: while your account is active, and for as long as needed for our accounts and legal obligations afterwards.
- Bank transfer screenshots: 90 days after review.
- Enquiries that don't lead to a booking: up to 24 months.

## 5. Cookies

We use essential cookies to keep you signed in, protect forms and remember your choices. These are always on.

With your permission, we also use analytics and advertising cookies from Meta (Facebook and Instagram) and Google Analytics to understand which adverts bring visitors and bookings. These only load after you click "Accept" on the cookie banner. You can change your choice at any time by clearing your cookies for this site.

## 6. Your choices and rights

- **Download your data:** from your account, you can download a copy of your information.
- **Correct your details:** update your profile and riders in your account, or ask us.
- **Delete your account:** from your account, or by asking us. We anonymise your information, except records we must keep for our accounts and the law.
- **Marketing:** we only send marketing messages if you opted in, and you can opt out at any time.

## 7. Security

We protect your information with encrypted connections, restricted access for our team with two-step sign-in, encryption of sensitive fields, and regular encrypted backups.

## 8. Children

Riders under 18 are booked by a parent or guardian, who provides their details and consent.

## 9. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated.
