## 5. Safety and riding rules

Horse riding involves risk, even when everyone is careful. Horses can move suddenly, and falls can cause injury. By booking, you accept these risks. To keep everyone safe:

- Bring your own properly fitted riding helmet and wear it whenever you are mounted. Wear closed shoes and long trousers.
- Tell us before your session about any medical condition, injury or pregnancy that could affect your riding.
- Follow the trainer's instructions at all times.
- Do not ride under the influence of alcohol or drugs.
- The trainer may refuse to let anyone ride, or end a session early, if they believe it is unsafe for the rider, other riders or the horses. If this is because a rider breaks these rules, no refund is due.
