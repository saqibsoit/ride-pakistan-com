## [Unreleased]

### Added
- Forced password change for team accounts:
  - `php artisan admin:create`, for a new or existing account, now marks the password as temporary (`users.must_change_password`).
  - Until it's replaced, the admin panel blocks everything else, including Livewire requests (`EnsurePasswordIsChanged`, persistent). The only things allowed are the "Change your temporary password" screen (`ChangeTemporaryPassword`, `ReplaceTemporaryPassword`) and signing out.
  - The new password must meet the password rules and differ from the temporary one. The change is audited without the value.
  - Filament's required MFA set-up follows.
- The admin panel path is `/admin` (`ADMIN_PATH=admin`) on staging and production (PLAN §7 and DEPLOY.md updated).
- Milestone 11 performance pass (`docs/PERFORMANCE.md`):
  - Measured before and after on every public page plus `/book` and checkout with discounts (Lighthouse mobile, built assets, HTTPS, median of 3). Performance is now 99–100 everywhere, LCP ≤ 1.9 s, CLS 0, TBT ≤ 122 ms (was up to 192 ms).
  - Front end:
    - shorter Alpine start-up task (no forced layout in the site header; tracking and attribution start later);
    - hero image preloaded with high priority and no opacity fade;
    - body font preloaded;
    - cover images prioritised;
    - Turnstile on the `/prices` form loaded on demand (nonce kept).
  - Full-page cache:
    - seat pages are versioned by a "seats version" instead of emptying the whole cache on every booking or hold (`PageCache`, `PublicPageHasher`);
    - campaign and click tags are ignored in the cache key;
    - no cached copies for signed-in visitors or visitors with flash data (`PublicCacheProfile`), which also fixes one-off tracking events being cached;
    - a fresh nonce on every cached hit (tested).
  - `Cache-Control: no-cache, private` on public HTML; long-lived headers for hashed build files and images (`public/.htaccess`); Cloudflare rules and staging checks in `docs/DEPLOY.md`.
  - Query budgets with an N+1 guard for every key page (`assertQueryBudget`), EXPLAIN checks for the booking queries, and a config-cache check. Fixed the booking flow's repeated product lookup (11 → 6 queries); review summaries are one aggregate query.

- Milestone 10e part B, discounts at checkout:
  - Pricing on the server (`app/Actions/Discounts`): `orders.subtotal_minor` (new, backfilled) is the price; `amount_minor` is now what is left to pay after discounts, stored as `order_discounts` lines. Order: promo code or the referral welcome discount (never both), then gift voucher, then account credit (`RepriceOrder`, `PromoEligibility`, `ReferralDiscount`). Percentages round down to whole rupees.
  - Checkout page: one field for a gift voucher or promo code, remove buttons, use or stop using account credit (`ChangeOrderDiscounts`, `checkout-discounts` rate limit). The open Stripe page is closed before the amount changes. The welcome discount applies by itself.
  - Voucher balance and credit are reserved when applied (`VoucherBalance::redeem`/`restore`, `AccountCredit::spend`/`giveBack`) and given back when an unpaid order ends (`ReleaseOrderDiscounts`, from `OrderStateMachine`); a voucher that can't take it back gives it as account credit (`voucher_returned`). Vouchers are locked before any plain read (MariaDB 1020) and their ids kept on the order, so parallel checkouts never deadlock.
  - Orders covered completely are confirmed with "Confirm booking" (`CompleteCoveredOrder`, payment method `discounts`; added to the allowed `FulfilOrder` callers in CLAUDE.md). Stripe gets one line for the amount left; card isn't offered below `STRIPE_MIN_CHARGE_PKR` [200].
  - Refunds give back the same share of voucher and credit as of the card money, from the admin and from the Stripe dashboard (`RestoreOrderDiscounts`); "Cancel and restore balances" for covered orders (`RefundCoveredOrder`). Referrers are rewarded for friends' bookings paid by voucher or credit.
  - Receipts, account and admin order pages, the data export, the orders export (subtotal, promo or referral, gift voucher, account credit, revenue) and the bookings export (price after discounts) show discounts. Revenue on the dashboard, lead reports and exports counts gift voucher spending (`OrderRevenue`). Referrals list the welcome-discount order.
  - Admin guide: "Discounts at checkout and refunds".
  - Migration: `add_subtotal_to_orders_table`.
  - Pest tests: amounts and rounding, every promo rule and limit (open and paid orders only), one promo per order, promo replacing the referral discount, stacking, voucher balance after partial use, expiry, release on expiry, referral abuse checks, Stripe line amounts and webhook matching, covered orders, proportional refunds (partial, full, rounding, expired voucher, dashboard refund, covered), reports and exports; a parallel-process race on one voucher; a browser smoke test.
- Milestone 10e part A, gift vouchers, promo codes and referrals (management; applying them at checkout is part B, after 10d):
  - Gift vouchers: `/gift-vouchers` (public, in the sitemap and footer) and `/gift-vouchers/buy` for signed-in customers: a product (its price at purchase) or a PKR amount, recipient, message, send now or on a date. Paid by card (own Stripe session, `metadata.kind = voucher`) or bank transfer (same proof, review and retention rules as orders), never through `CreateOrder` or `FulfilOrder`. `HandleStripeEvent` routes voucher sessions and refunds to `HandleVoucherStripeEvent`; amount, currency and reference are re-checked. `IssueVoucher` creates the voucher only once payment is confirmed (idempotent, 12-character code, `vouchers.validity_months` [12]).
  - Delivery: recipient email with the code and a signed printable voucher page (`/vouchers/{code}`), straight away or at 09:00 Pakistan time on the chosen day (`vouchers:deliver`); receipt to the buyer, and a note when a scheduled voucher goes out. The buyer can change the recipient until then (`/account/vouchers`). `vouchers:release` closes unpaid purchases; `vouchers:expire` writes off expired balances. Every balance change has a ledger entry (`VoucherBalance`).
  - Promo codes (admins, `SavePromoCode`, `ArchivePromoCode`): percentage or fixed PKR, products, window, total and per-customer limits, minimum order, first booking only; terms fixed once used, archived instead of deleted; usage and discount given per code.

  - Admin (Sales & promotions, admins only): Gift vouchers (issue by hand with a reason, void, extend, resend, change recipient, history), Verify voucher transfers, Voucher purchases (review flags), Promo codes, Referrals (reject, reinstate); Account credit on the customer page (adjust with a reason).   - Pest tests: amounts and limits, send dates, Stripe amount and metadata, webhook issue once (redelivery, async success), amount/currency/reference mismatch, late and cancelled payments, failed and expired sessions, Stripe refunds, transfer upload/replace/approve/reject/expiry and proof access, delivery timing in Pakistan time, printable page signing, recipient changes, expiry and extension, emails rendering, privacy; promo validation, locking, archive/delete, status labels, admin forms; referral capture and attach, abuse matrix, rewards (paid, pack, refunded, free, no-show, once), credit adjustments, admin access.
